Tacoma FIRST 311 policy (summary in our own words of https://tacoma.gov/services/tacoma-first-311-services/, checked 2026-11-09)

For an emergency, call 911.
Tacoma FIRST 311 means Fast Information Resource Service Team.
Inside Tacoma city limits, call 3-1-1. From anywhere, call (253) 591-5000.
You can report online 24 hours a day at tacoma.gov/311 or with the Tacoma FIRST 311 mobile app.
The Tacoma FIRST 311 desk is at 747 Market Street, 2nd floor. It is open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., except on holidays.
Tacoma FIRST 311 is the best way to ask for a city service and follow its progress.
